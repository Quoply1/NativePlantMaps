# Provider images

Use a verified photo of the nursery first, its authentic logo second, and a plant photo published by the nursery third. Inspect the image before assigning it. Avoid portraits, group photos, and stock photos of gardeners. Do not substitute generated graphics, generic text, or third-party certification seals for nursery logos. When no suitable image can be confirmed, leave the image absent and keep the official website link.
